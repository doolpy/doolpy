# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Inferred from the request: Korean-speaking people playing Go-Stop together who want to calculate a round's score without interrupting play.

## Product Purpose

Inferred from the request: provide a practical, mobile-friendly Korean-language Go-Stop score calculator with clear per-player totals.

## Positioning

Inferred from the request: a focused scoring flow that makes score components and rule choices visible instead of presenting an unexplained total.

## Operating Context

Inferred from the request: players enter a round's scoring categories and go count while playing in person, often on a phone. Scores may need correction or a safe reset.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Requested: account for common 광, 열끗/멍따, 띠, and 피 scoring categories, go count and bonus multiplier effects, and per-player totals. Support safe edits, undo, or reset. Make rule variations transparent and do not present one house-rule set as universal.

Open decision: exact scoring rules and optional bonuses vary by group. The app should label its default assumptions and expose relevant switches rather than imply a universal rule.

## Evidence on Hand

No existing product assets, scoring dataset, or brand materials were present in the repository. Do not imply official or universal rules.

## Product Principles

- Show how a total was calculated.
- Keep score entry quick on a phone.
- Make player identity and round ownership explicit.
- Surface rule assumptions at the point they affect the result.
- Make changes reversible and resets deliberate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Inferred from the mobile-friendly request: controls should have clear Korean labels, adequate touch targets, visible focus, and totals that do not rely on color alone.
